SETTLED (from Matan, 17 Nov)
- The child does the thinking. The game never reveals the answer and never does her inference steps or removals for her.
- Slower and more deliberate than today's help: more explanation and more child input. Low text, visual links between clue and grid.
- Entry: a quiet button at the edge of the question card, and a stronger "solve together" offer after a mistake. (Both entry points were chosen for guided help in general; the idle timing is not settled, see PROPOSED/OPEN.)
- Validation of marks (Matan said yes, 17 Nov ~15:54, to the proposal described to him as per-step validation supported by the clues): each mark she confirms is checked at that step against what the clues support, not only against the final answer. The exact wording of what was put to him was given by voice; the details below are not settled.
- Interface: one card per kid. Each candidate (pet, fruit) is a picture, not a word. A candidate can be marked "is" or "isn't".
- The guide shows how each clue maps to a mark and guides the next inference.
- After she confirms an ownership or preference, the guide prompts her to remove the same option from the OTHER kids (each option belongs to one kid). She makes those removals herself.
- Hebrew, right to left, same visual style as the rest of the game.
